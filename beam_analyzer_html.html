Concepts Used
  • • Buckling is a sudden instability of a slender column under compression (L/D > 10).
  • • The buckled state has lower energy: if bending energy < compressive energy, the column buckles.
  • • Fixed–free: EI y″ + P y = P yL, so y = yL(1 − cos(√(P/EI) x)) and Pn = [(2n+1)π/2]² EI/L².
  • • n = 0 gives Pcr = π²EI/4L². Higher modes only occur if the lower ones are restrained; between two modes no buckled profile exists.
  • • Pin–pin: π²EI/L². Fixed–fixed: 4π²EI/L² (shapes follow from the same method).
  • • Pcr ∝ 1/L² and Pcr ∝ EI, with I = πd⁴/64 (circle) or bh³/12 (rectangle, weaker axis).
  • • Fracture: bending stress σ = P/A + Mc/I with M = EI y″ (flexure formula) reaches the yield strength.
